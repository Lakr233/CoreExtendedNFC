Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Overview

This app is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. All information stays entirely on your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. Specifically:

- No analytics or usage tracking
- No crash reporting to external services
- No advertising identifiers
- No network requests of any kind
- No user accounts or registration

NFC Data

This app uses your device's NFC reader to scan contactless smart cards, tags, and electronic travel documents. All scanned data — including card identifiers, memory contents, NDEF records, and passport information — is processed entirely on your device.

Scanned data is never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party. No network connection is required or used during or after scanning.

Local Storage

Scan records, memory dumps, NDEF records, and passport records are stored as files in the app's local document directory on your device. Every successful card scan, memory dump, and passport read is saved automatically so it appears in the app's lists; NDEF records are saved when you create, scan, or import them. These files:

- Remain on your device; the app never uploads them
- Are accessible only by this app (protected by the iOS app sandbox)
- Are protected by iOS Data Protection and cannot be read while your device is locked
- Can be exported or deleted by you at any time (swipe a record or use Delete in edit mode)
- Are automatically removed when you uninstall the app

Scan, dump, and NDEF records are included in your device's iCloud or computer backups like other app data. Passport records are excluded from backups and never leave your device unless you export them yourself.

Files you export are written to a temporary location, protected by iOS Data Protection, and deleted as soon as the share sheet closes. Exported passport files are named "Passport-" followed by a random identifier, without your name or document number. Data you copy from passport screens stays on this device (it is not shared through Universal Clipboard) and is cleared from the clipboard after two minutes.

Sensitive Information

When reading electronic travel documents (e-passports), the app may process sensitive personal information such as name, nationality, date of birth, document number, and facial photograph as encoded in the document's chip. This information is:

- Processed locally on your device only
- Never transmitted over any network
- Saved automatically as a passport record after each successful read, so you can view it again later
- Protected by iOS Data Protection and excluded from iCloud and computer backups
- Hidden from the app switcher while a passport screen is open
- Removed when you delete the record or uninstall the app

The document number, date of birth, and date of expiry you type to unlock a chip are used only to authenticate with the chip and are not saved separately from the passport record. Passport records imported from a file are labelled as imported; their security checks are not shown as passed because they were not performed on your device.

Encryption

This app uses standard cryptographic algorithms (AES, 3DES, SHA) solely for authenticating with NFC chips and verifying document integrity as required by international standards (ICAO 9303). No encryption is used for network communication, as the app makes no network connections.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. There are no external dependencies that collect or process your data.

Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be included in the app update. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed accordingly.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store support link.
